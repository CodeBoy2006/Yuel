# Yuel Product Brief

## Audience and purpose

Yuel serves Chinese-speaking learners and knowledge workers who read long-form English documentation, PDFs, and articles in a desktop browser. They need to understand difficult passages, track a sustainable reading habit, and revisit useful vocabulary or excerpts without breaking their reading flow.

The product combines in-flow assistance with local, engagement-gated reading estimates. Success means a reader can resolve a difficult passage, understand today's progress, and continue reading without feeling watched or scored. Current capabilities and operating limits belong in the [README](README.md).

## Product priorities

1. **Keep the reader in flow.** Assistance and saving should stay close to the passage; reflection belongs in the dashboard.
2. **Make estimates understandable.** Distinguish measured attention, first reading, and review from comprehension. Explain unavailable or insufficient evidence at the point of use.
3. **Make each surface useful.** The popup controls, the side panel accompanies, the PDF reader supports document work, and the dashboard supports reflection.
4. **Keep the habit sustainable.** Make daily state legible without pressuring the reader to interact or compete.
5. **Keep data under the reader's control.** Store reading history locally, make recording controls and deletion accessible, and explain retained source metadata and browser-service dependencies.

## Personality and boundaries

Quiet, trustworthy, and attentive: a precise reading instrument and a calm companion. Compact enough to disappear into the task, warm enough to acknowledge meaningful progress.

Avoid competitive fitness rings, gamified streak pressure, scoreboard-style hero metrics, noisy productivity dashboards, decorative green washes, and continuous celebrations that obscure reading state. Local analytics should not become social performance.

## Inclusion

Support keyboard use, readable contrast, non-color state cues, and reduced motion. Apply the concrete [accessibility requirements](DESIGN.md#accessibility-requirements) across all surfaces.
