# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Chinese-speaking learners and knowledge workers who read long-form English documentation, PDFs, and articles in a desktop browser. They use Yuel between paragraphs to understand difficult passages, see what they have genuinely read, maintain a sustainable daily habit, and revisit useful vocabulary or excerpts without breaking reading flow.

## Product Purpose

Yuel is a local-first browser extension that combines in-flow reading assistance with measured, engagement-gated activity to produce trustworthy daily progress, page coverage, reading-rhythm insights, vocabulary exposure, and saved excerpts. Success means users can resolve a difficult passage, understand today's state immediately, and continue reading with confidence rather than feeling watched or scored.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, trustworthy, and attentive. The interface should feel like a precise reading instrument and a calm companion: compact enough to disappear into the task, but warm enough to acknowledge meaningful progress.

## Anti-references

Avoid competitive fitness rings, gamified streak pressure, scoreboard-style hero metrics, noisy productivity dashboards, decorative green washes, and celebratory effects that run continuously or obscure the reading state. Do not make local analytics look like social performance.

## Design Principles

1. Make state legible in a glance without demanding attention.
2. Reward genuine reading progress, not compulsive interaction.
3. Let each surface serve its context: control in the popup, accompaniment in the side panel, reflection in the dashboard.
4. Prefer measured evidence and honest system feedback over decoration.
5. Keep personal reading data local, calm, and under the user's control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and meaningful states. Never rely on color alone: progress and completion need accessible names or supporting text. Preserve complete keyboard focus treatment and usable touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences by replacing animated transitions and glow pulses with immediate, static state changes. Ensure progress remains distinguishable for common forms of color-vision deficiency.
