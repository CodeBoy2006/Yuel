# Contributing to Yuel

Bring fixes, tests, documentation, and improvements back to [CodeBoy2006/Yuel](https://github.com/CodeBoy2006/Yuel). For a substantial change, discuss the problem in an issue before investing in an implementation.

## Prepare and submit a contribution

1. Read the [license](LICENSE). Its upstream contribution permission allows a working fork, local changes, builds, tests, and upstream review. It does not authorize an independent edition or public release of your build.
2. Create a focused branch in your contribution fork. Identify the fork as unofficial and link to the upstream project. Retain the licensing files and third-party notices.
3. Follow the [upstream development guide](https://github.com/CodeBoy2006/Yuel/blob/main/docs/development.md), add relevant regression coverage, and describe what you verified. Keep generated binaries out of the pull request and do not publish fork releases, packages, or extension-store listings. Share a review build only with the people reviewing or testing the contribution.
4. Identify copied or adapted third-party material, its origin, and its license. Do not submit material you lack permission to contribute. Obtain any necessary employer or co-author authorization first.
5. Open a pull request against the official upstream project. Read the agreement below and explicitly confirm acceptance using the PR checkbox or this statement in a comment from your own account:

   > I have read and agree to the Yuel Contributor License Agreement 1.0 in CONTRIBUTING.md for my contributions in this pull request, and I have authority to grant its rights.

Each contributing author must confirm for their own contribution; a PR author cannot accept for someone else without authority. A sign-off or an existing GitHub account alone is not this confirmation. For a submission outside a PR, send the same acceptance with a precise identification of the submitted material.

You may abandon an experiment or retain a closed PR and its source history as an inactive contribution archive. An unmerged patch does not become permission to maintain or distribute a separate product. You remain free to reuse your own independent work, excluding Yuel code and other people's material.

## Maintainer merge checks

Before merging, verify acceptance of Contributor License Agreement 1.0 from every relevant rights holder or authorized representative, and check third-party provenance and license compatibility. Retain the acceptance comments and the applicable agreement revision in the PR record; request renewed confirmation if the agreement changes. The PR template records acceptance manually; it is not an automated merge gate.
